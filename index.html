Who We Are

A Place Where Knowlwdge Meets Community

Greenwood Public Library has been a center of knowledge, creativity, and community for over four decades. Our mission is to make learning accessible to everyone, regardless of age or background.

From timeless literature to modern technology, our library offers a wide collection of books, digital resources, and educational materials. We provide a peaceful envrionment where readers can explore new ideas and expand their knowledge.

Beyond books, Greenwood is a place where people come together. Through workshops, reading programms, and community events, we encourage creativity, curiosity, and lifelong learning.

Whether you are a student, reasearcher, professional, or simply someone who loves reading, our doors are always open, Greenwood Library welcomes everyone to discover, learn, and grow.
